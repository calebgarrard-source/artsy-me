A family studio since 2001

  1. 2001

    2001. Cricket and Brad Willis open Artsy Me on Walton Way Extension on December 1, three weeks before their fourth child is born. It is Augusta's first contemporary ceramic studio.

  2. 2012

    2012. After a move to Davis Road and years of taking pottery to schools, churches and events with the kids in tow, the studio opens in Evans in February. 4275 Washington Road is one of the oldest landmarks left in Columbia County, and it is still home.

  3. 2016

    2016. A second studio opens in downtown Augusta in June.

  4. 2020

    2020. The downtown studio moves to Grovetown. Artsy Me @ the Gateway opens its doors on October 1.

  5. Now

    Now. A third studio is open in Apalachicola, Florida. The hope is the same as on day one: a safe, creative place for people of all ages.
